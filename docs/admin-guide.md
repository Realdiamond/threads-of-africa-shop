# Admin guide

For the owner and team. Everything is at **`/admin`** (sign in with the email and password you were given). The dashboard opens with a **launch checklist** (until everything is done), **orders to ship**, anything **needing attention**, and stock warnings.

## Orders

`Orders` lists every order. Filter by status or search by order number, name or email.

| Status | Meaning | What you do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Paid — to ship** | The customer has paid. | Pack it, then open the order and press **Mark as shipped** (add the carrier and tracking number; the customer is emailed). |
| **Shipped** | On its way. | Press **Mark as delivered** when it arrives. You can correct the tracking details at any time. |
| **Awaiting payment** | The customer is on the payment page. The items are held for them. | Nothing. If they don't pay, the order is cancelled and the items are released automatically after about 40 minutes. |
| **Cancelled** | Not paid; items released. | Nothing. |
| **Refunded** | Money sent back. | Nothing. |

- **Refund** (owners only): open the order → *Refund this order…*. Choose whether the items go back into stock (on by default only if it hasn't shipped). The customer is emailed.
- **Private note**: only the team sees it.
- **Resend receipt**: if a customer lost it.
- **Download as spreadsheet** (owners only): all orders or the filtered view, ready for accounting or the courier.
- Orders that need a person's attention appear at the top of the dashboard — a payment for the wrong amount, or a customer who paid after the hold expired and some items had since sold out. Open the order; its history explains what happened.

## Products

`Products → Add a product`: title, descriptions, **Details & fit** and **Fabric & care** (shown on the product page), category, tags (the first tag shows on the card; use `new arrival` for the *New arrivals* filter), sizes with price and stock, and photos (the first photo is the main one; drag-free ordering by the arrows; large photos are shrunk automatically).

- Set the status to **Active** to put it on sale, **Draft** to keep it hidden, **Archived** to retire it.
- A new **category** (for example *Kids*) appears in the shop filters as soon as it has a live product; products in a category called *Kids* are also shown on the Kids page.
- Sold-out sizes disappear from the size picker; low stock is listed on the dashboard (threshold in Settings).

## Shipping and tax

`Shipping & tax` (owners): create **zones** (a list of countries, or "everywhere else"), the **delivery options** for each (price, optional free-delivery threshold, delivery-time text) and the **tax rate** for the zone. Customers only see countries you deliver to. Any change asks you to confirm again, so a typo is never silently live.

## Pages, settings, messages

- `Policy pages`: edit privacy, terms, shipping and returns. Simple formatting: `## Heading`, `**bold**`, `- list`, `[link](https://…)`.
- `Settings`: shop name, contact email, WhatsApp, social links, announcement bar, low-stock threshold.
- `Messages`: contact-form messages (they also arrive by email). Reply by email, then mark handled.
- `Subscribers`: newsletter sign-ups; owners can download the list.
- `Activity`: a log of who changed what.
- `Team`: add staff (they can handle orders, products and messages; only owners can refund, change prices of delivery, edit policies and manage the team).

## Customers

Customers can check out as guests or create an account (order history, saved details). You don't manage accounts: they choose and reset their own passwords. If someone can't get in, they use **Forgot your password?**; if the email never arrives, check that the dashboard shows "Sending emails" as done.
